🔒 DATA PRIVACY & FRAUD PROTECTION

Privacy Policy & Security Protocols

Last updated: September 2025 • PW Premium Hub Educational Portal

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase a batch pass or claim referral payouts on PW Premium Hub, we only collect necessary identifiers: Student Name, WhatsApp/Mobile Number, Email Address, and UPI Reference/UTR number. We never store your bank passwords, debit/credit card CVVs, or biometric authentication details.

2. Payment Security & Fraud Prevention

All transactions are routed directly through standard NPCI UPI protocols (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM). Every UTR transaction reference number and payment slip uploaded is checked by our verification team to prevent duplicate submissions, fraudulent claims, and ensure verified student access.

3. Zero Spam & Data Confidentiality

We operate a strict Zero Spam policy. Your phone number and email are solely utilized to send you your order verification status, login access credentials, and course updates. We never sell, rent, or trade student personal records with commercial third-party ad networks.

4. Referral System Integrity

Referral payouts are disbursed directly to the verified UPI ID provided by the referrer. In case of fraudulent order creation, fictitious UTR generation, or spoofed referral links, the platform reserves the right to void illegitimate commission claims.

5. Contacting Data Protection Desk

If you have questions regarding your data or wish to delete your transaction record, you can connect directly with our data desk via WhatsApp at any time.
