Effective Date: September 2025 • PW Premium Hub

1. Service Description & Delivery

PW Premium Hub provides instant access to curated competitive exam passes (IIT-JEE, NEET UG, Foundation, NDA, and Commerce). Upon completing UPI payment and submitting your 12-digit UTR reference or payment receipt, our verification team checks and unlocks your batch access on the secure app portal within approximately 10 to 15 minutes.

2. Payment & Verification Process

Payments must be performed using UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) to the verified payee: Aditya Kumar Dubey. Submitting incorrect, forged, or unconfirmed UTR transaction numbers will result in delayed activation or order cancellation.

3. Refund & Replacement Policy

Because batch passes grant digital material and unlisted video lecture access, purchases are generally final once delivered. However, if you experience technical issues accessing your batch within 24 hours of payment and our support team is unable to resolve it, a replacement batch or full UPI refund will be provided.

4. Refer & Earn Guidelines

Commission payouts (30% to 50%) are calculated based on genuine student enrollments. Self-referrals with the same contact or fraudulent order IDs will not be approved. Valid claims are transferred directly via UPI within 1-2 hours of order verification.

5. Student Support Assistance

For any queries regarding order status, portal login, or course material, our WhatsApp helpdesk is active daily from 8:00 AM to 11:30 PM.
